## Original practice added

Twenty independently authored, four-choice short questions have been added, addressing:

- Equal de Broglie wavelength and the electron/proton speed ratio, including **order of magnitude**.
- Wave speed determined using a `π/2` phase difference across a quarter wavelength.
- Photon frequency from a transition energy originally expressed in eV.
- Receiving-aerial alignment for vertically polarised radio waves.
- Consecutive string harmonics inferred from changing node intervals.
- The area under force–time data as momentum impulse.
- Free fall from rest: speed proportional to the square root of displacement.
- Elastic energy of a uniform rod expressed through Young modulus, area and length.
- Kirchhoff's first law with several currents entering and leaving a junction.
- Total up/down quarks in a nucleus from its constituent protons/neutrons.
- Comparing drag forces when two skydivers have different masses but each reaches terminal speed.
- Wavelength in a refractive medium from its refractive index and the air wavelength.
- Identifying the SI base units of a newton, distinct from the units of energy.
- Recognising that Young modulus is unchanged for wires of the same material and conditions.
- Calculating power dissipated in one parallel resistor from the common p.d.
- Cylindrical rod mass scaling with the **square** of diameter.
- Steady engine input power after accounting for efficiency.
- Daughter nucleus composition following beta-plus decay.
- Real voltmeter input resistance calculated from a parallel-loading circuit.
- Climbing-car power, including `mg sinθ` and resistance along the slope.

All are original numbers, prompts, correct answers and distractors. They are **not** the AQA 2025 items, diagrams or mark-scheme text. They remain outside the school's mock-only preset (`mock:false`).
