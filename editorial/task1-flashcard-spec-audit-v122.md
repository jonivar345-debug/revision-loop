# Task 1 — Flashcard specification audit and release ledger (v122)

**Date:** 26 October 2026  
**Status:** Proposed flashcard content release; publication only after CI and merge.  
**Course:** AQA Physics 7408, Chemistry 7405, Mathematics 7357.

## Count and preservation

- Existing IDs retained: **1220**. No original IDs have been deleted or renumbered.
- New short-recall cards: **378**; Physics **155**, Chemistry **213**, Maths **10**.
- Bank after merge: **1598** total, **1594** active (four inactive legacy cards retained).
- **35** new cards deal with written assessment of practical interpretation and measurement.
- **34** new cards include existing built-in labelled SVG explanations on the *answer* side.
- The new cards have brief prompts and answers (front <=170 characters, back <=205 characters). All new IDs are prefixed `task1_`, all reference an AQA section in `specRef`.
- A semantic-duplicate review removed **40** proposed cards whose points were already tested elsewhere. Existing cards such as key Hess's law definitions were not replaced by redundant copies.

## What was deliberately not attempted

- **No claim of fully certified line-by-line specification completeness yet.** Syllabus headings are covered more evenly, but every small assessed bullet still needs an individual tick-off against the precise AQA document, especially untaught A2 content and Medical Physics option.
- No copying of official AQA question text or mark schemes; these are original recall prompts.
- No long multi-step questions in the flashcard bank. These belong in exam practice.
- No revision history, XP, points, purchases, profiles, globe photographs or cloud account data modified.
- No new paid services or external image dependencies: diagrams reuse existing site SVGs.

## Follow-up quality work after this release

1. Audit **each** AQA subsection against cards, particularly Physics 3.7–3.10 and Chemistry 3.2.4, 3.2.6 and advanced Organic chapters. Keep untaught topics out of the November mock queue.
2. Expand high-value AS practical recall without turning flashcards into unsafe procedure instructions or full method questions.
3. Improve acceptance of short synonymous answers and observe genuine student marking results; creating a card does not prove the answer checker awards fair marks.
4. Inspect diagrams with actual browser screenshots where possible. Existing site SVG drawings are reused, not newly generated photo assets.
5. Record further specific card gaps in this ledger rather than inflate deck numbers with near-duplicates.
