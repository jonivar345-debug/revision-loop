## Five selected method families per paper

### 2018 — four written/practical methods and one MCQ

- **Q01.1:** Light-gate speed uses card length divided by the *beam-interruption time* at each gate; acceleration uses the *between-gate time*. New card `as_p2_v445_001`.
- **Q01.2:** To compare two gate separations, take the average of the initial/final speeds and multiply by between-gate travel time. A longer time alone does not establish longer distance. `002`.
- **Q02.3:** An annular magnet's volume uses the **difference of the squared diameters** times π/4 and thickness, followed by cubic unit conversion. `003`.
- **Q03.4:** The number of charge carriers in a brief detector pulse follows `Q=IΔt` and `N=Q/e`, including all SI-prefix conversions. `004`.
- **Q06 MCQ:** Alpha emission reduces nucleon number by four; beta-minus does not change nucleon number. Count alpha decays from the nucleon-number difference. `017`.

### 2019 — measurements, electrical graphs and particles

- **Q01.1:** A distance measured from two separate ruler readings inherits uncertainty from **both** positions, including identifiable spot-centre uncertainty. `005`.
- **Q01.2:** For the sum of two lengths, **add absolute uncertainties first**, then express their sum as a percentage of the total length. Averaging two percentage uncertainties is not valid. `006`.
- **Q02.3:** On a curved I–V graph, the actual resistance at a particular point is `V/I`; using the gradient of a tangent in this question was not credited. `007`.
- **Q03.2:** Photon momentum follows `p=E/c` after converting MeV to joules; `p=mv` is inapplicable. `008`.
- **Q05 MCQ:** Young modulus and stress both have dimensions of pressure, `kg m⁻¹ s⁻²`; energy, force and torque do not. `018`.

### 2020 — refraction measurement and emf graphs

- **Q01.4:** Difference between two travelling-microscope scale readings has absolute uncertainty equal to the **sum** of the absolute uncertainties of the readings. `009`.
- **Q01.5:** For a refractive index determined as a ratio of two measured differences, percentage uncertainties add; the mark scheme permits correctly constructed upper/lower bounds too. `010`.
- **Q02.2:** On a falling terminal-voltage/current graph, the **minimum gradient magnitude** is the shallowest straight line that remains compatible with the error bars (not the steepest negative line). `011`.
- **Q02.4:** Use a plotted V/I point and internal resistance in `ε=V+Ir` to obtain emf; distinguish intercept from gradient. `012`.
- **Q05 MCQ:** Order SI prefix multipliers by their actual powers of ten; in particular pico, nano, micro and milli. `019`.

### 2021 — nodes, uncertainty, extension and inclined forces

- **Q01.3:** A distance between two measured node positions inherits both endpoint uncertainties; divide the total absolute uncertainty by the full span for percentage uncertainty. `013`.
- **Q01.4:** The distance between the first and ninth minima contains **eight** intervals each of λ/2; hence `λ=2d/8` before using `f=c/λ`. `014`.
- **Q02.1:** A suspended mass displaces the midpoint of a wire vertically, but extension must come from the geometry of **both slanted halves**, not the vertical sag alone. `015`.
- **Q04.2:** On a stationary sloping conveyor, friction parallel to the surface balances `mg sinθ`; it is not the perpendicular normal reaction. `016`.
- **Q06 MCQ:** A neutral atom gaining electrons forms a negative ion. Estimate specific charge using `q/m`, signed charge `−ne` and the approximate total nucleon mass. `020`.
