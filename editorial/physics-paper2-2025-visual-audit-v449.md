**Important source-correction:** The v448 2025 index row 08 previously used AQA 3.2.1.5 (particle classification). The relevant AQA AS specification is **3.2.1.4 (particle interactions)**, which explicitly covers electron–proton collisions, electron capture and W⁺/W⁻ bosons. Do not teach a virtual photon as the mediator of electron capture. Electromagnetic *elastic* electron–proton scattering is a different reaction and is not equivalent to this source topic.
