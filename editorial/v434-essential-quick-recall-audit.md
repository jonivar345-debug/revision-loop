# v434 essential quick-recall audit (AQA 7408 / 7405 / 7357)

Date: 10 November 2026. Scope: **existing original short flashcards only**, with no content replacement, new IDs or new user-progress schema.

## Why these facts were added

The v429 prioritisation began with definitions and selected unprinted equations; v433 added AQA Chemistry identification/test observations. The original core bank still included fundamental **Physics graph interpretations, required-practical relationships, uncertainty rules, and Maths exact results/identities** that were not included in priority-only revision. The user asked to prioritise similarly essential short recall, not to turn all exam working into flashcards.

## What is selected

- **28 Physics facts**: signed area/gradient on s–t, v–t and a–t graphs; I–V and EMF-gradient interpretation; required-practical 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 graph/gradient relations; percentage/absolute uncertainty rules; Newton's-third-law pairs; impulse; phase/interference and photoelectric graph results.
- **26 Maths facts**: proof-by-exhaustion definition, discriminant interpretation, circle tangent and perpendicular gradient facts, basic differentiation/integration, logarithm and trigonometric identities, exact values, graph reflections, vector components and the binomial coefficient.
- **2 Chemistry mass-spectrometry facts**: interpretation of electrospray protonation and `[M+H]⁺` peak mass, supplementing the v433 simplified EI/ESI equations.
- Curated on **verified stable IDs**, not loose keyword matching. Other cards and valid multi-step questions stay in ordinary practice. A fact's label **does not claim its equation is absent from the AQA formula booklet**. The classifier still excludes `formulaBooklet:'provided'` and `memoryPriority:'provided'` from priority.
- Distinct badges: `Key graph / practical fact`, `Essential maths recall`, `Key Chemistry recall`, plus v433 `Chemical test to know` and the original definition/equation badges.
