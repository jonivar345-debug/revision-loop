# Revision Loop — destination photograph collection

The public World Library preview has been retired. Countries, oceans and seas are still saved as source-backed research files and are shown in the **main website**, after the matching destination has been unlocked and its globe pin selected.

## Destination list

See `editorial/world_photo_collection_template_376.csv`, containing:
- 158 distinct existing places (three pairs of duplicate historic IDs use one canonical record)
- 196 country gateways
- five oceans
- 17 seas

There are **376 displayable destinations** with **379 underlying stored IDs**. Do not rename IDs in the CSV or the website: purchases use them.

## Five photos per destination

Choose a **recognisable landscape or landmark** for Photo 1 (also used as the shop-card thumbnail). Then choose contrasting photographs of the streets/people/culture, physical landscape, history/architecture, and a distinctive feature. Avoid screenshots, diagrams, maps, watermarks, unrelated stock imagery, duplicates, or pictures that could depict the wrong place.

Collection can be done in batches. Prioritise London, Busan, Tbilisi, Tokyo, Seoul, Hong Kong, Singapore, Sydney, New York City and the most-used destinations before attempting every country.

For each picture supply either:

1. A direct **Wikimedia Commons File:** page link, which lets us check the author's name and licence and attribute the photograph correctly; or
2. Your **own original photograph**, provided you permit its publication on the website; or
3. A file accompanied by a link and clear permission allowing publication and editing of size/format.

Do not assume an image found in Google Images, Instagram or Pinterest may be copied to a public GitHub Pages site. The page must retain attribution and licence links when required, even though they can stay inside a small expandable **Photo credits** disclosure.

A useful folder structure for approved original files is:
```
world-photos/
  london/
    01-overview.jpg
    02-culture.jpg
    03-landscape.jpg
    04-history.jpg
    05-detail.jpg
  busan/
    ...
```

Use the `Location ID` column to prevent confusing similarly named destinations. A single ZIP containing a batch of location folders is convenient, or fill the Photo 1–Photo 5 columns with direct Commons **file page URLs**. We will validate files and captions before adding them to the actual photo gallery. The spreadsheet itself does not grant copyright permission.
